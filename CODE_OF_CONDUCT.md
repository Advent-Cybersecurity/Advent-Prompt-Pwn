# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, harassment-free community for everyone participating in the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific in technical disagreement.
- Separate evidence from assumptions.
- Protect assessment targets, customer information, and vulnerability details.
- Credit researchers and prior work.
- Accept correction and improve the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, unauthorized targeting, credential sharing, and publication of sensitive assessment material are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or disruptive. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method listed on the Advent Cybersecurity website.
